# LAR_PH_001 — location brief

**Canon location:** M-01 (Mount Larp / Larpova hora), western massif  
**Camera station:** hiking trail TR-04 (ridge M-01–M-02), a few dozen metres south-west of the summit, standing eye level.  
**Coordinates (atlas km, origin NW of 24×16 km frame):** camera ≈ x 6.90, y 5.52; summit M-01 = 6.96, 5.44; elev_model summit 560.0 m; camera ~530–545 m.  
**Heading:** ~255–270° (west / west-south-west) toward the Pacific over descending forested slopes.  
**Status:** existence of the 560 m western peak is approved; height, trail geometry and camera station are proposed.

## Nearby (must respect)

- M-02 ~2.2 km east (behind camera if looking west — do not show a higher peak in front).
- M-03 ~3.1 km SW (may be a rounded forested shoulder at left if heading WSW; max 440 m, not alpine).
- N-01 cliff lookout ~4.2 km west at ~15 m a.s.l. — rocky coast, no beaches on this NW cliff coast.
- V-02 cliff viewpoint under M-01 is much lower (~141 m) and is NOT this shot.
- Ocean is close (~4 km west). Do not show Paraland (SE), Larpington (SE ~11 km), extra islands, or alpine ranges.

## Terrain note

Highest land in the country is 560 m. This is a forested coastal hill-massif with late-winter snow *patches*, not a snow-capped alpine peak. Visibility of the sea from near-summit looking west is plausible (downslope to cliffs). Exact line-of-sight was not ray-traced on the DEM in this pass (PNG DEM is present but treated as off-chat binary); claim is directional, not surveyed.
