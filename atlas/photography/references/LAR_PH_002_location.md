# LAR_PH_002 — location brief

**Canon location:** RD-R1 (approved coastal ring road) at the eastern edge of T-02 Cimprlichovo Hradiště, looking toward C-01 Larpington.  
**There is no registered V- viewpoint “above Larpington”.** This shot uses a proposed roadside pull-off on the existing R1, not a new named POI.

**Camera station (proposed):** x ≈ 12.40 km, y ≈ 11.70 km, elev_model ~50–70 m (T-02 is 56.3 m).  
**Heading:** ~110° (east-south-east) toward C-01 (16.08, 12.16, 18.2 m), distance ~4.6 km.  
**Behind camera:** western forested hills rising toward M-03/M-02; those hills must stay modest (≤560 m) and mostly out of the night sky as dark silhouettes, not alpine walls.

## In frame

- Foreground: dark two-lane R1 asphalt, a safe paved pull-off, a black-and-gold Koenigsegg Jesko parked legally beside the road (fiction; no real partnership).
- Mid: forested roadside, maybe a simple timber fence or low stone wall, no crash-stunt composition.
- Background: compact warm cluster of Larpington lights in a low coastal basin; harbour C-02 slightly right/further south; no skyscrapers; mountains not behind the city (hills are west, behind/left of camera).

## Avoid

- Inventing a cliff-jump or racing shot.
- Showing Paraland as a second lit island (it is SE of the capital, ~8–12 km further; at night from T-02 it should not read as a separate archipelago).
- Mega-city skyline, snow-capped Alps, extra islands.
